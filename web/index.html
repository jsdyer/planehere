How to read the sky

Bearing is the compass direction from your viewpoint to the last reported aircraft position. Track is the direction the aircraft is moving. The map points north at the top.

Reported barometric altitude is an aviation measurement, not height above your roof or local ground. A type such as E295 is an aircraft model code. Route origin and destination are not inferred.

Freshness, coverage & privacy

Area observations are shared for up to five minutes to stay within the free data allowance. This page does not poll in the background. Positions older than 15 minutes are hidden. Check each aircraft’s age before interpreting its location.

Lookups send an area rounded to 0.1° to CoolCode and adsb.lol. OpenStreetMap receives map tile requests. Saving a viewpoint is optional and stores its exact coordinates only in this browser. Use Forget saved viewpoint to remove it.

This is a public discovery tool, not an aviation navigation or safety service.
